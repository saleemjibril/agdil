---
title: "Empowering Women Farmers to Improve Nutrition in Nigeria"
description: Gender-intentional agrifood programmes improve household nutrition and incomes.
date: "2025-01-15"
---

Women play a central role in **food production**, **processing**, and **child feeding** decisions. Investments that expand women farmers’ access to inputs, finance, and markets often translate into **better nutrition** at household level.

Policy and programme design should reduce unpaid care burdens, improve land and finance access, and connect women-led enterprises to **value-add** opportunities.
