title: "Feeding the Future: Why School Feeding and Nutrition Education Must Go Hand in Hand in Nigeria"
description: School feeding programmes and nutrition education together improve child outcomes.
date: "2025-01-15"
---

School feeding is one of the most practical ways to improve **dietary diversity** and **learning outcomes** at scale. When paired with **nutrition education**, children, families, and communities build habits that last beyond the lunch line.

In Nigeria, aligning menus with local agrifood systems can strengthen **smallholder markets** while addressing **micronutrient gaps**. Programmes should be designed with gender-sensitive implementation and clear accountability for food safety.
