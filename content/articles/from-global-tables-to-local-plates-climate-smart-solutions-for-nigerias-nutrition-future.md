---
title: "From Global Tables to Local Plates: Climate-Smart Solutions for Nigeria’s Nutrition Future"
description: Climate-smart agrifood practices supporting nutrition resilience.
date: "2025-01-15"
---

**Climate shocks** disrupt production, prices, and diet quality. **Climate-smart** crop choices, water management, and post-harvest innovation can stabilise supply of nutrient-dense foods.

Localising solutions—rooted in **Nigerian agroecologies**—helps communities adapt while protecting livelihoods.
