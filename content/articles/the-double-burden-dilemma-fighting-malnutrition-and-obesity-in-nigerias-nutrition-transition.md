---
title: "The Double Burden Dilemma: Fighting Malnutrition and Obesity in Nigeria’s Nutrition Transition"
description: Addressing undernutrition and overweight together in a changing food environment.
date: "2025-01-15"
---

Nigeria faces a **double burden**: persistent **undernutrition** alongside rising **overweight and obesity**, driven by urbanisation, ultra-processed foods, and shifting livelihoods.

Responses require **food environment** policies, **social protection**, and **agrifood systems** that make healthy diets accessible and affordable—not only more calories.
