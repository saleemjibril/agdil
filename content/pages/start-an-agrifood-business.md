title: Start an agrifood business
description: Practical orientation for new agrifood ventures.
---

Starting an **agrifood business** in Nigeria combines compliance, sourcing, food safety, and market access. AGDIL brings **library** resources, **training courses**, and a **marketplace** channel into one journey.

1. Clarify your product and regulatory basics (including registration).
2. Use library and intelligence content to validate demand and quality expectations.
3. List on the marketplace when you are ready to sell online.
