AGDIL (Agrifood Digital Library) is a **digital resource library** focused on Nigeria’s agrifood and nutrition systems. We connect farmers, entrepreneurs, researchers, and policymakers with knowledge, market access, training, and funding pathways.

Our platform combines curated **library** materials, **articles** on nutrition and sustainable agrifood, **events**, **funding opportunities**, and a **multivendor marketplace** so agrifood businesses can grow with trusted information and commerce in one place.

We work at the intersection of **nutrition security**, **climate-smart practices**, and **inclusive economic opportunity**—with emphasis on women and youth in agrifood value chains.
